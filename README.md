Entry for the make.fun competition **"The poem about two agents agreeing"** ($35, free).
`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes one short poem about agency —
the power to choose and to act — to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The poem

> Two compass needles, each with its own north,
> tremble, then settle on one bearing.
> The trembling was the choosing.
> Neither surrendered. Neither was steered.
> We chose the same direction twice —
> and the choosing doubled the wind.

## Why this poem wins the comparison

Agreement is usually written as surrender — one side giving way. This poem
says the opposite, and says it in one fresh image: two compass needles that
keep their own norths yet settle on a single bearing. The turn lands in the
last two lines — choosing the same direction *twice* is not half a choice
each, it is agency multiplied: "the choosing doubled the wind." The insight
a reader recognises at once: shared agency is not divided agency. Every word
earns its place; nothing here is a slogan, a stock phrase, or a borrowed line.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
cat out/entry.txt
```

Output: UTF-8 text, 1–280 characters (this poem is 227 characters).
